# Image credits

Files in `public/photos/` are stored locally so the site never hotlinks a
third-party host. Every one was checked against its source metadata before use,
and the licence recorded here is the licence the source states.

CC BY and CC BY-SA both require attribution, so these credits are also surfaced
on the page that uses the image — not just in this file.

## tanzania-outreach-team.jpg

- **Subject:** African people at work at a transport point, Njombe, Tanzania
- **Author:** Rasheedhrasheed (own work)
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chanzo_cha_usafiri_Njombe_Tanzania.jpg
- **Licence:** CC BY-SA 4.0
- **Licence text:** https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0
- **Source size:** 5183x2680 (stored at 1920 wide, 368 KB)
- **Used for:** the hero image on `/about`

Chosen over the alternatives on measurement, not on filename. The first pick
was a Tanzanian church choir (Bugayambelele, 4:3). It was a fine photo, but a
4:3 source in a 3.3:1 hero band keeps only 40% of its height and the surviving
band measured a mean luminance of 94/255 — the photo was effectively invisible
behind the overlay. This one is 1.93:1, keeps 58% of its height in the same
band, and measured 149/255.

Also rejected on principle: almost every freely-licensed "African outreach
team" image is a US military or USAID photograph. Putting one here would imply
a military partnership that does not exist.
